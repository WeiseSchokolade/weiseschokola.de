About me

Hi. I'm WeiseSchokolade, a programmer/gamer/creator/computer science student from Germany.

I grew up with several different contact points to computers, technology and engineering in general.

I like tinkering with code and working on projects that help or entertain other people.

I'm a fan of maths, rocketry and engineering and greatly enjoy learning that sort of stuff.

However ☝️ I don't want to only focus on that. I've found great enjoyment in all kinds of sciences, politics, in the arts, literature, theater and music.
